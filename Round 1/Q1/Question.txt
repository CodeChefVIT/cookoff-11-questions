Cave-Chef is preparing a grand feast for his prehistoric tribe. He has spent the morning foraging and hunting, lining up a sequence of $N$ wild ingredients on a long stone slab.Each ingredient $i$ in the sequence has a flavor score $A_i$. A positive score indicates a mouth-watering addition, like roasted mammoth flank or wild honey. A negative score represents a foul-tasting item, like bitter roots or poisonous moss, that will ruin the dish.Cave-Chef wants to build a giant roasting skewer by sliding a straight, continuous sequence of these ingredients onto a sharpened bone. To ensure the tribe enjoys the feast, he needs to select a contiguous segment of ingredients (containing at least one item) that yields the maximum possible sum of flavor scores. Help Cave-Chef find the maximum flavor score he can achieve.
